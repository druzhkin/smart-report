## Live Q3 dry-run results

**Critical regression vs baseline. Routing assumption proven wrong on
the first live test.**

| Metric | Baseline (Step 3.3) | Config B (hybrid live) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | $0.89 | **$1.58** | +78% |
| Sources in final | 29 | **2** | -93% |
| STRONG claims | 64 | **0** | -100% |
| MODERATE | 1 | 0 | -1 |
| WEAK | 1 | **20** | +20 |
| SPECULATIVE | 1 | **3** | +3 |

Stop-condition `cost > $1.50` BREACHED ($1.58, +$0.08).
Stop-condition `evidence_quality drops below baseline` BREACHED
catastrophically (every claim downgraded).

### Root cause

Valyu's `search_type="proprietary"` for an EU-regulatory query
returned **arXiv + PubMed** academic papers, NOT eur-lex / cinea /
europa primary documents. The 7 Valyu hits in this run all came from
the academic-research datasets, because that's what "proprietary"
ACTUALLY means in Valyu's corpus today: "all our curated datasets,"
which is dominated by arxiv/pubmed/financial/biomed and contains no
EU-regulatory primary corpus.

Day 1 capability map already noted this (`docs/VALYU_CAPABILITY_MAP.md`:
"No EU regulatory dataset listed by name"). Day 3 routing built
EU_REG → Valyu proprietary anyway, on the brief's assumption that
Valyu would cover EU regulators. Day 4 dry-run is the first
empirical disproof.

### Why source count collapsed 29 → 2

Of the 7 Valyu hits, the source-quality classifier (Step 3.3)
correctly graded most as WEAK or SPECULATIVE — a Q4 2025 arxiv
preprint about DAC chemistry is genuinely WEAK evidence for a
regulatory question. The synthesizer then dropped most of them from
`all_sources` as below-threshold for citation. Only 2 made it
through (one arxiv, one pubmed link).

The intake pipeline also stripped most of the markdown adapter's
content because the URLs aren't in any AUTHORITATIVE_DOMAINS_BY_QUERY_DOMAIN
registry for EU_REGULATORY (which expects europa.eu / eur-lex / cinea
/ etc.).
