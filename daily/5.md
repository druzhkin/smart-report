   counts on Valyu coverage that doesn't exist for `regulatory_eu`
   and `financial_global`.** That's actually fine under v3 §3.4
   degradation design — every such call WILL trigger DOCX warning,
   making the gap visible to users. Just budget for it.

2. **Day 4's regression now has a documented root cause** in the
   capability map: Valyu has no eur-lex / europa dataset.

3. **The standard recon was less informative than the brief assumed.**
   For Valyu self-description meta-queries, you get web-search of
   their own marketing. The free `datasources()` call gives the
   authoritative corpus list. The standard tier's value is for
   actual research queries (not seen yet).
